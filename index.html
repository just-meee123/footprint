1

Real account details, in the currency you're paid in

Get dedicated details for USD, EUR, GBP, MXN and COP. Ask a client or employer to send a normal bank transfer — no crypto knowledge required on their end.

2

A rate and a fee you see before you confirm

Swap fiat for crypto inside your balance at a flat, disclosed fee. No spread hidden in the exchange rate, no surprise deduction after the fact.

3

Send to any wallet, on the network you choose

Withdraw BTC, ETH, USDT or USDC to an external address. Pick the network yourself, and every request is checked by a real person before it's sent.

Pick the network when you withdraw.

Choose an asset to see where it can be sent, what the address looks like, and the network fee for each withdrawal.

What it costs.

One flat conversion fee, plus the network's own fee when you withdraw. Both are shown before you confirm.
